# Ideas

An idea raised for later goes into `docs/ideas.md`, through `/velzimar:idea`, and stays there until it is grilled.

- An entry is the idea and its open questions, with where it touches the glossary and ADRs. It is not a design.
- Before adding one, check the plan and the phases: a part a phase already covers stays with that phase, and anything it leaves open becomes an open question in that phase's file.
- An idea joins a phase only after it is grilled (see [Grilling](grilling.md)).

**Why:** ideas come up mid-task; writing them down keeps the task focused without losing them, and keeping them apart from the plan stops untested ideas from passing for decisions.
